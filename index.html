Our Legacy & Vision

Over three decades of architectural splendor and warm hospitality

Born from an Uncompromising Passion

In the spring of 1987, pioneering hotelier Margaret Thornton envisioned a haven that blended European residential warmth with uncompromising grandeur. Starting as an exclusive 40-key private residence, Grand Horizon quickly gained global acclaim.

Today, our 200-room property is an architectural landmark, celebrated for timeless neoclassical design, hand-carved Italian marble corridors, and world-class hospitality.

We pride ourselves on our enduring pledge: "Every guest is family, and every moment is an unforgettable memory."
